How it works

01 · Friday

Design it, print it

Pick a template, add your name and a QR to whatever you want to share. Send the file to a print shop near you.

02 · Shabbat

Hand it over

Paper only. Slip it to the person you clicked with between the soup and the dessert.

03 · Motzei Shabbat

They scan, you're saved

Once Shabbat is out, they scan the QR. One tap and you're in their phone.
